# Chvmp Streetwear Market

Build a mobile-first ecommerce prototype for CHVMPBUZZ, a streetwear brand from Cairo, Egypt, for men and women.

Image 1 is the layout reference. Follow its structure. Image 2 is the brand mood. Use only its colors and logo style, not its layout.

BRAND
- Tagline: "Vague Vibes Everyday."
- Slogans: "Believe. Build. Become." and "More than fashion."
- Est. 2024

STYLE
- Minimal streetwear: black and white base, red as the only accent
- Colors: black #000000, white #FFFFFF, light grey #E6E5E2, red #D10F18
- Use red sparingly: the asterisk mark, logo, small highlights, hover states
- Logo: red CHVMPBUZZ wordmark, with BUZZ in a white pill and an asterisk circle, as in Image 2
- Bold clean sans-serif (Inter or similar), huge tight headlines, lots of white space
- Sharp corners, no gradients, no heavy shadows

SECTIONS
1. Announcement bar: "Cash on delivery across Cairo and Giza"
2. Header: logo centered, links Men / Women / New in, search, cart
3. Hero: giant CHVMPBUZZ wordmark with the model overlapping it, like Image 1. Buttons: Shop now, Explore new in
4. Black strip with two blocks: Men and Women
5. Story banner: "Believe. Build. Become." plus a short brand paragraph
6. Trust row: Fast delivery, Order on WhatsApp, Cash on delivery, Easy exchange
7. Best sellers grid: hoodies, tees, jeans, caps, with color swatches and size picker
8. Shop page with Men / Women filter
9. Footer: WhatsApp +20 155 382 9354, chvmpyymacavelly@gmail.com, Snapchat chvmpbuzz

CART AND CHECKOUT
- Cart drawer with quantity controls
- Checkout form: name, phone, city, address, payment (Cash on delivery, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash)
- On submit, show an order number and a "Continue on WhatsApp" button that opens wa.me/201553829354 with the order details prefilled
- Currency: EGP

Use placeholder product images for now. Keep the design simple and fast.
